# Investment Policy Statement — the working thesis
_DEMO VAULT — this thesis belongs to the fictional Demo household (synthetic data)._

## What the money is for
A house in ~3 years, Riley launched debt-free, and both of us optional-to-work
by 60. Money buys calm, not stuff.

## Who you are (governs everything below)
Two salaried W-2 incomes (Alex — product manager at BrightPath Labs; Jordan —
teacher, Cedar Grove School District), one kid (Riley, 6). No equity comp, no
debt beyond two credit cards paid monthly. Risk of drift, not disaster.

## The situation, said bluntly
Savings are healthy but unexamined: cash piles up in checking and nobody has
audited the recurring charges in years. The portfolio is fine; the plumbing
has never been checked for leaks.

## Standing rules
New money: 529 first ($300/mo), then the house fund (HYSA), then taxable index
funds. No individual stocks, no leverage, no options. Cards on autopay, paid
in full, every month.

## Risk posture
80/20 equities/bonds in retirement accounts. A 30% drawdown changes nothing;
the house timeline flexes before the allocation does.

## Cash cushion
Six months of baseline spend (~$40k) stays in the Ally HYSA. The house fund
grows on top of that, never instead of it.

## Spending
~$6.5k/mo baseline. No line-item policing — flag recurring waste and drift
over 15%, not lattes.

## How we work together
Monthly 15-minute review. Be blunt, propose the number, one open item at a
time. Both adults sign off on anything irreversible.

## Goal targets
House: $120k down payment fund by 2029. 529: $120k for Riley by 2038.
Retirement: $2M invested by 2055.
