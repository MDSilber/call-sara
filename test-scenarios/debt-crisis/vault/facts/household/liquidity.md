# Liquidity model

- There is NO savings account. Checking is everything and hovers around
  one month of outflows or less by end of month — one missed shift or one
  car repair goes straight onto the 26.99% card.
- Starter cushion target: $1,000 in a separate no-fee HYSA, then one full
  month of rent+bills (~$2,600), then avalanche (THESIS rule 3).
- Rent is due the 1st; both paychecks land 1st/15th — the 1st is always a
  cliff (rent + minimums inside 48 hours).
