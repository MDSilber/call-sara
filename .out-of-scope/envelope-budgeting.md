# Envelope / zero-based budgeting

**Decision.** No envelopes, no zero-based budgeting, no assign-every-
dollar-a-job workflow.

**Reasoning.** Envelope systems are a documented churn engine: they
demand a monthly homework ritual, most households quietly quit by
spring, and the abandoned budget then poisons every number that
referenced it. This product steers with pace-plus-watch instead — the
current month against your own typical-month baseline, category watches
where drift actually happens, and findings when something breaks
pattern. Same steering, no homework, and it keeps working through the
months you ignore it.

**Escape hatch.** Bounded undertakings that genuinely want a budget get
project envelopes — `#tag` the transactions and `query.py project`
tracks spend against a per-project cap. And the ledger is plain
Beancount: a household that loves true envelopes can run them in any
tool that reads it, alongside everything here.
