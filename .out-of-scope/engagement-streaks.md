# Engagement streaks

**Decision.** No streaks, no badges, no daily-open pressure, no "you
haven't checked in" guilt. Ever.

**Reasoning.** A streak is a retention mechanic wearing a wellness
costume — it optimizes for the app being opened, not for the money
being right. Manufactured guilt is the one tone a trusted advisor can
never take, and a system that needs your daily attention to stay
correct is broken anyway; this one reconciles and raises findings
whether you look or not. Celebrations here mark real milestones — a
goal hit, a debt gone, a fee clawed back — which is exactly why they
land.

**Escape hatch.** If a household wants rhythm, the honest versions
exist: the weekly letter, the monthly review, and a cadence line in
THESIS.md that every session honors. Rhythm the household chose, never
a widget's leverage over them.
