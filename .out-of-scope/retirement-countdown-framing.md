# Retirement-countdown framing

**Decision.** No surface in this product renders a walk-away date, a
FIRE countdown, a coast number, or any "you could quit in N years" hero.
Not on Sara Home, not in the app, not in the letter.

**Reasoning.** A countdown quietly converts a money dashboard into a
verdict on your working life, and it only fits households that already
frame their money that way. For everyone else it's someone else's
obsession rendered in your hero slot — it nags, it moralizes, and it
crowds out the question these pages exist to answer: how are we doing,
and what needs us this week. Glanceable surfaces carry verdicts the
whole household signed up for; a retirement clock isn't one.

**Escape hatch.** The math is not banned — the framing is. A household
that wants walk-away numbers can ask Sara in chat any time; the
forecast, the thesis, and `finance_calc` make it a two-minute computed
answer with its assumptions on the table. If it matters durably, write
the target into THESIS.md as a standing goal and it'll be advised like
any other goal — it just won't become a wall clock.
